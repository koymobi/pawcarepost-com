About pawcarepost.com

Paw Care Post is the editorial brand of pawcarepost.com. We publish plain-English guides about dog care, cat care, pet health, food and nutrition, and life with pets. Our aim is to help readers ask better questions, build sensible everyday routines, and recognize when a veterinarian should be involved.

Editorial independence

We are not a veterinary clinic and do not offer individual diagnosis or treatment. A link to a veterinarian, professional association, pet food brand, or other resource does not mean that organization sponsors or endorses this site. We do not claim affiliation with third-party services or use unauthorized APIs to obtain protected content. Where we cite outside guidance, we identify the source and add our own explanation for readers.

How we work

Articles are published under the Paw Care Post Editorial Team name. We aim to distinguish general guidance from urgent warning signs, avoid unsupported certainty, and revise material when a correction is needed. For personal medical decisions, a qualified veterinarian who knows your pet is the right source of advice.

Questions or correction requests can be sent through our contact page. Our medical disclaimer explains the limits of information on this site.
