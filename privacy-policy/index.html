Privacy Policy

How pawcarepost.com handles site visits, contact messages, and optional services.

Draft for review before public launch. This page describes the current static preview; it must be updated if the site adds services that process personal data.

Site identity and contact

This website is presented under the Paw Care Post brand at pawcarepost.com. Privacy questions and requests to access or correct information may be sent to hello@pawcarepost.com. The same address appears on our contact page.

What the current site handles

The site is a collection of static pages. Search runs in your browser against a local article index. The newsletter signup is not connected in this preview, so entering text there does not send it to us. There are no visitor accounts, payments, analytics scripts, or advertising scripts active in the current version.

A future hosting provider may receive ordinary technical requests, such as an IP address, page requested, browser information, and request time, in order to deliver the site and protect its systems. The provider, log retention, and any international transfers must be confirmed for the live deployment and reflected here before launch.

Purpose, sharing, and retention

Messages sent to our public email address may be used to respond to enquiries and handle corrections. Providing an email address is voluntary, but we cannot reply to a message without it. The email provider may process those messages for delivery and storage. We do not currently operate a mailing list or sell visitor information through this preview. Our exact retention period and provider details need to be confirmed for the live service.

Cookies, advertising, and your choices

The current site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. If advertising or analytics is added later, this policy will name the services, their purposes, applicable consent controls, and how to change your choice before those tools are enabled. If Google advertising is ever used, you can manage Google ad personalization through Google My Ad Center (turn off personalized ads) and read how Google uses information from partner sites. These links do not mean Google ads are active here today.

You can also use browser settings to manage stored cookies. External links on this site open third-party resources with their own privacy practices.

Privacy rights and applicable law

Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance may apply to personal data handled in Hong Kong. It provides rights to request access to and correction of personal data. Depending on where readers live and how the live site operates, other laws, including the GDPR or Brazil's LGPD, may also apply. Send a privacy request to hello@pawcarepost.com; we will handle it under applicable law. The legal framework, responsible data user, and operating arrangements must be confirmed before launch.

Children and changes

Our articles are written for general pet owners, not specifically for children. This preview does not intentionally request personal information from children. We will update this page when the site's data practices change and show the current version here.
