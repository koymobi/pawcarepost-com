Medical Disclaimer

The limits of general pet health information on Paw Care Post.

For education, not diagnosis

Content on Paw Care Post is for general informational and educational purposes only. It cannot replace veterinary diagnosis, treatment, or professional veterinary advice. Reading an article does not create a veterinarian-client-patient relationship.

Pets can show similar signs for very different reasons. Your veterinarian can examine your pet, consider its history, and determine the underlying cause. Do not delay seeking care because of information on this site.

When to seek help

Contact a qualified veterinarian if your pet has a new, persistent, worsening, or unexplained symptom. Seek urgent veterinary care for trouble breathing, collapse, seizures, severe pain, suspected poisoning, major injury, or other alarming changes.

Never start, stop, or change a prescribed treatment based only on a website article.
